# Raspberry Pi Note Manual

## Connect to Raspberry Pi

### SSH

1. Open powershell or command prompt on your computer.
2. `ssh hai1@raspberrypi.local` (replace `hai1` with your username on Raspberry Pi)
3. `002456` Enter your password when prompted.

### RealVNC

1. `raspberrypi.local` (replace `raspberrypi.local` with your Raspberry Pi's hostname or IP address)
2. `hai1` and `002456` Enter your username and password when prompted.

### Raspberry Pi Connect

You can use the Raspberry Pi Connect app to connect to your Raspberry Pi. Download it from the official website and follow the instructions to set it up.
